# Ain't No Floor

## intro (spoken, low, over the break)
Ladies, gentlemen, and everybody in between:
the floor has left the building.

## a1 (verse, chanted on the riff)
Somebody opened the basement door,
and the bottom fell out of the bottom floor.
Mister Landing in his rented shoes
says come on down, you got nothing to lose.

## b1 (chorus, call and response)
Ain't no floor! (ain't no floor!)
Ain't no floor! (ain't no floor!)
We ain't coming down, we ain't coming down.

## a2 (verse)
Grandma's falling in a velvet chair,
reading the paper in the open air.
The horns keep blowing at the passing ground,
and the bass is the only one that still knows down.

## brk (breakdown, call and response)
Hey, Mister Landing! (what?)
Where's the ground? (we lost it!)
Who's got the bottom? (nobody!)
Then nobody lands!

## b2 (chorus)

## a3 (over the horns)
Fall in, fall out, fall up, fall down,
fall in, fall out, fall all the way round.
Nobody lands tonight.

## end
Floor!
